## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an **AI Engineer & Data Scientist** based in **Bengaluru**, with an MSc in Data Science from Christ University and around 2 years of professional experience.

I work on building practical AI and ML solutions, including LLM-powered applications, RAG pipelines, and agentic workflows. I enjoy taking ideas from experimentation to working applications that solve real business problems.

- 🔭 Currently building **Generative AI and agentic AI applications** at **DataNeurus**
- 🌱 Exploring **multi-agent systems, LLM evaluation, and MLOps**
- 💬 Ask me about **Python, Machine Learning, RAG, LLMs, and FastAPI**
- ☁️ Working with **GCP, AWS, and cloud-based application deployments**
